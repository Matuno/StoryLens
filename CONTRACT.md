# Story Lens contract

Status: **DRAFT / experimental**. This standalone source export preserves the original workspace behavioral contract and verification history. References to the workspace registry, promotion directories and bridge describe that development environment; those files are not required to build this repository. Publishing source does not complete the outstanding personal release checks.

## Identity and purpose

- Internal name: `StoryLens`
- Owner: Detle
- Purpose: Shows quest objective overlays and uncertain story-progress clues for visible players, with persistent last-seen points for manually tracked players and the full Free Company roster.

## Inputs and triggers

A manual name/home-world tracked list, explicitly added player target, or selected session-only observation. Framework samples of all valid spawned players exposed by the local object table, excluding the local player; quest/Level sheets, positions and public OnlineStatus. The user's 2026-10-15 instruction explicitly authorizes preemptive session-only observation of all visible players and persistent last-seen data only for tracked players.

## Outputs and persistence

/storylens opens the tracked and searchable session-only lists, last-seen details, ranked evidence, quest search, world-overlay controls, and Quest chain & duties tab. The chain follows a focused quest or the selected player's strongest candidate, with up to three previous/five upcoming quests and duties. Configuration schema 3 persists the tracked list, preferences, and tracked last-seen UTC time, territory/map/instance, world position and up to 128 clue references/timestamps. Session-only players never enter configuration. Adding a tracked player adopts their existing session evidence. New tracked snapshots checkpoint every 30 seconds, with immediate saves for explicit tracking/settings changes and flushes on logout/unload. Schemas 1 and 2 migrate without losing the list/preferences. Chain browsing adds no persisted player data and never asserts quest completion.

## Client-state and unload behavior

Pause reads/overlays and break observation continuity on logout, zoning, instance change, duty, PvP, GPose and local cutscenes. Logout retains in-memory session observations and flushes tracked data. Unload flushes tracked data, clears the ephemeral cache, and removes commands, framework/UI events and both IPC gates. A single cancellable HTTP operation may run off-thread; unload cancels and awaits it before disposing HTTP resources. Native pointers are never retained. Saved clues never seed live actor/status continuity, so a reload cannot invent a fresh cutscene transition.

## Privacy and retention

All spawned player identities/positions and bounded clue sets may be retained in RAM for this running plugin session, as explicitly requested. Manual players and verified FC members may persist locally. The current user instruction authorizes automatically reading their public Lodestone profiles. Public character/FC Lodestone IDs, profile freshness and progression evidence are saved locally. No content/account IDs, telemetry, identity logs or identity/position export through IPC. Stopping manual tracking deletes the saved point unless the player is also an automatically tracked FC member; ordinary session-only observation continues. Clearing recorded data deletes that player's saved/session evidence; enabled observation can gather new data afterward. Pause stops collection. Session-only data is discarded on unload/game exit and can be evicted at the documented capacity limit. No historical location trail is written; tracked records are a single last-seen snapshot plus bounded quest clues.

## Explicit non-goals

No exact remote quest-state access or claim of completion. No remote invisible NPC recreation, gameplay automation, input synthesis, memory writes, hooks, arbitrary commands or network service. Only verified FC membership automatically enrolls session players in persistent tracking. No private-profile bypass, login cookies, external API service or cross-client sharing.

## Inference and data assumptions

- Catalog: all named quests with nonempty internal IDs; issuer locations plus all nonzero valid TODO Level references. Labels use TODO index + 1; completion sequence is stored as metadata and is not presented as another player's current sequence.
- `Level.X/Y/Z`, `Territory` and `Map` supply world coordinates and scope. NPC labels resolve type-8 objects through `ENpcResident`. Story phasing never controls whether an overlay may be shown.
- MSQ browsing classification uses `JournalGenre.Icon == 61412`, checked against the installed data. This filter does not remove side quests from ambiguity calculations.
- The cutscene online status is resolved by the English `OnlineStatus` label `Viewing Cutscene` from installed sheets. Missing/ambiguous status metadata fails plugin initialization; there is no guessed offset or raw native fallback.
- Samples occur every 250ms for all valid spawned players exposed by the object table, with no additional distance cutoff. A match is within 6 horizontal and 4 vertical yalms, in the local territory and map. Continuity additionally requires the same zone instance and actor ID, nondecreasing time and a gap at most 1 second. A cutscene-start clue requires a false-to-true edge and displacement at most 3 yalms. All observations are heuristic, including apparently unique catalog matches.
- Linger evidence requires 8 seconds of continuous proximity. Repeated samples do not accumulate votes. Evidence is ranked by observed cutscene entry, cutscene already present, linger, then proximity; there are no calibrated probabilities or assertions of quest completion.
- Cap: 32 manually tracked players plus up to 512 FC members, 4,096 session entries and 128 clues per player. Oldest untracked entries are evicted at capacity; tracked entries are protected. Clues do not expire after 20 minutes: that window gives recent observations ranking priority, then strength/time. A new visit after the window replaces the old clue for the same objective. Last-seen and clue timestamps remain distinct, and saved/absent observations are labelled explicitly. Invalid configuration fails initialization without rewriting. Save failures remain visible and retry after 30 seconds; a crash can lose the latest checkpoint interval.
- The overlay is an ImGui projection of managed positions, without depth/wall occlusion. It shows at most 32 candidate markers per frame within 150 yalms, suppresses nearby screen overlaps, and never edits native map markers, NPCs or quest state.
- Chain metadata includes all named quest records with nonempty internal IDs, even without coordinates. `Quest.PreviousQuest` provides predecessors; reversed edges provide successors. Traversal stays within MSQ or other quest kind, stops at three predecessors/five successors, and never arbitrarily chooses at a fork. Branches, alternate versions, missing data, ends and cycles are surfaced. Cross-kind prerequisite quests are listed separately. The view does not infer player completion from chain position.
- Duty associations use `Quest.InstanceContentUnlock`, `Quest.InstanceContent`, quest-linked `ContentFinderCondition.UnlockCriteria/UnlockCriteria2` with unlock type 1, and numbered `INSTANCEDUNGEON` / `QUESTBATTLE` script symbols. InstanceContent resolves to ContentFinderCondition; QuestBattle IDs at least 5000 resolve through InstanceContent, older IDs through QuestBattleResident.SoloDuty with a generic fallback for valid unnamed QuestBattle records. Duty names/types come from sheets, with a generic category for blank type labels. Duplicate associations merge their reference/unlock/prerequisite labels. Unknown symbols are not interpreted as duty IDs. Links may include optional/alternative requirements, retired versions or incomplete coverage; they are not assertions of entry/completion or exhaustive duty requirements.

## Free Company extension (2026-10-15)

The user explicitly requests automatic progress tracking for all members of their Free Company, public mount/minion/achievement checks, combat-level ceilings and location clues. This supersedes the earlier manually selected-only persistence and no-network scope.

- Schema 3 preserves manual players and last-seen points. FC records are separate from the 32-entry manual list and may contain up to 512 validated name/world/public-Lodestone-ID pairs. Configuration validation permits saved points only for that union. Newly enrolled FC members adopt existing session clues. A complete verified replacement roster removes departed members' saved points unless manually tracked; failed/partial roster reads preserve the previous roster. Switching local characters requires verifying the new roster before replacement. Debug mode defaults off.
- Native reads: `InfoProxyFreeCompanyMember.Instance()`, `FreeCompanyId`, matching `InfoProxyFreeCompany.Id/TotalMembers` (coverage diagnostics), inherited `EntryCount`, `CharData`, and each cached `CharacterData.NameString/HomeWorld/Location/State`. Guards require a pointer, a count in 1..200, and the local character's name/home-world in that cache before trusting the company. Copy immediately to managed values, without reading content/account IDs. Offline/disconnected entries do not supply locations. The live experiment confirmed the local cached Location equals public ClientState.TerritoryType. Cached locations are supporting evidence, not a fresh server observation; record time is the time this plugin first saw that cached value change. No request-data functions, list paging actions, signatures, hooks or memory writes.
- Complete membership comes from all public Lodestone `/freecompany/{id}/member/?page=N` pages, validated before atomic replacement. Native caches cover at most 200 members. A missing native cache falls back to exact local-character name/home-world Lodestone search, then its profile FC link. Zero/ambiguous/private results do not choose another character.
- A single HttpClient connects only to `https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com`, using GET, no cookies and no redirects. Paths are constructed from validated numeric IDs and escaped search text. One outstanding request, at least 5 seconds between dispatches, 20-second request timeout, 4 MB response limit, one-second regex timeouts. Requests operate on managed strings only. Private-section HTTP 403 responses with a matching character frame and no Retry-After are parsed as unavailable and do not halt the queue. Other HTTP failures preserve evidence, back off at least 10 minutes globally and six hours for the affected member, respecting a longer Retry-After in either date or delta form. Existing persisted deadlines are honored: the older format did not distinguish server-requested delays from local fallback. The framework reads public InCombat and all three BoundByDuty conditions each 250ms scan. Combat, any duty, an unavailable local identity or the FC toggle being off pauses dispatch, results application and cached FC reads before accessing that work. An already sent HTTP request may finish into its bounded buffer; it is processed after resuming. Pause preserves the section cursor and global deadline, so resume cannot bypass backoff or send a catch-up burst. Unload cancels and awaits the outstanding operation without framework dispatch.
- Roster freshness is six hours; a character's four-page scan covers class/jobs, mounts, minions and Main Scenario achievements (category 76). Completed scans are stale after three days, including inaccessible sections so privacy does not trigger repeated retries. Section cursor, freshness, evidence and global next-request deadline persist. Saving uses the existing 30-second checkpoint; interruption can repeat at most the uncheckpointed work. Only stale members are scheduled, with partially processed members first. All section outcomes are shown independently.
- Progress comes from English quest metadata: MSQ genre icon 61412, JournalGenre + SortKey, ClassJobLevel[0], explicit launch/patch grouping. `ShB - 80%` means approximate position through the Shadowbringers launch catalog; patches have a separate label. A unique linger/cutscene quest candidate yields `~`; a verified collection/achievement completion yields a minimum (`+`); territory-only hints yield `?`. Alternate city routes and legacy records make early ARR percentages approximate; these are not actual quest-completion counters. No arbitrary midpoint or calibrated probability is invented. Completed unlock floors cannot be lowered by replay/old observations. Newer area clues can suggest a later stage, with the known unlock floor retained in the explanation.
- Seven curated, untradable direct quest-reward collections are configured in ProgressCatalog. Each rule is checked against the installed Quest reward and Item metadata at catalog load. Official Lodestone acquisition icon hashes match only the collection list, not sidebar/news images. Main Scenario achievements use explicit “Complete the main scenario quest” descriptions and matching Quest keys, and only `data-achieved="1"` counts. Missing/hidden items never imply incomplete quests. See EVIDENCE.md for sources and exclusions.
- A complete DoW/DoM profile section supplies maximum normal combat-job level, excluding crafting/gathering and Blue Mage. It creates an upper constraint at the first higher-level quest, never a lower bound; an old snapshot (three days) stops restricting new evidence. Conflicts with completion evidence are surfaced. Current equipped job level is not used as a maximum.
- Territory hints use the earliest MSQ with an objective in the exact territory ID. This includes The Ocular (844); shared names, access, revisits and New Game+ cannot establish a unique quest phase. Quest objective matches retain the existing finer coordinate/height/ambiguity rules. No assumption that an inaccessible/phased NPC is present is made.
- The read-only smoke adds progress-catalog coverage and aggregate roster/profile/request counters. It emits no identity, Lodestone ID, location or collected-unlock data. Registry risks now record ClientStructs and outbound network use. No release/promotion is authorized by this extension.
